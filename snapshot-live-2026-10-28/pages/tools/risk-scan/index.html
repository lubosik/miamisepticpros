Why Check Your Septic System Risk?

Septic system failures can cost $5,000 to $15,000 or more in emergency repairs. Early detection of risk factors can help you avoid costly backups, health hazards, and property damage. Our free risk scan uses Miami-Dade County DERM data and analysis of 10,000+ system assessments to identify potential problems before they become emergencies.

What You'll Get

  • Instant Risk Score (0-100): Understand your system's overall risk level
  • Personalized Risk Factors: Specific issues that increase your risk
  • Next Actions Checklist: Steps to reduce risk and prevent problems
  • Estimated Repair Costs: What you could face if issues are ignored

Common Risk Factors

  • System age (15+ years increases risk)
  • Overdue pumping or maintenance
  • High water table (common in Miami-Dade)
  • Drainfield issues or compaction
  • Tree roots near system components
  • Heavy water usage or garbage disposal use
