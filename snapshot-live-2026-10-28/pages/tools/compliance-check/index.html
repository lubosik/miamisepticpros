Why Compliance Matters in Miami-Dade

Miami-Dade County has strict septic system regulations to protect public health and the environment. Non-compliance can result in fines up to $500 per day. Our free compliance checker helps you identify issues before inspection, sale, or renovation.

What You'll Get

  • Compliance Status: Pass, Fail, or Warning
  • Specific Violations: Exact issues that need to be addressed (if any)
  • Fix Checklist: Step-by-step requirements to achieve compliance
  • Fine Risk Assessment: Likelihood and potential cost of non-compliance fines

Common Compliance Issues

  • Type 1 system installed after January 1, 2023 (now prohibited)
  • Missing or inadequate secondary treatment (Type 2 required)
  • Insufficient setbacks from property lines or water sources
  • Improper drainfield design or installation
  • Missing permits or documentation
  • System modifications without DERM approval
