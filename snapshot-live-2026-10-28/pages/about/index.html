Who We Are

Miami Septic Pros is owned by First Class Contractors, a United States Dispatcher of Contractors across several home-based industries, providing numerous services including Septic services and Sitework services. Miami Septic Pros is a subset of First Class Contractors, specializing in Septic Installation and Repair services throughout Miami-Dade County.

We operate as a service-area business, meaning we dispatch licensed contractors directly to your property. Our Miami address serves as our dispatch office—we don't operate a walk-in storefront, but we're always available by email and online to schedule service and answer your questions.

Our Services: We specialize exclusively in high-ticket septic services—Septic Installation and Septic Repair. We do not offer low-ticket services such as septic inspections or routine maintenance.

Important: We do not offer 24/7 emergency services. All service requests should be made during regular business hours.

How Our Service Model Works

Miami Septic Pros operates as a service-area business (SAB). Here's what that means for you:

When you contact us, we handle the logistics, scheduling, and customer support. We then dispatch a licensed contractor from our network directly to your property to perform the work. This model allows us to:

  • •Match you with the right contractor for your specific job—whether you need a simple pumping, a complex installation, or emergency repair.
  • •Ensure contractors have the correct equipment and certifications for your service type.
  • •Provide faster response times by routing jobs to the nearest qualified contractor.
  • •Maintain consistent quality standards across all services through our contractor vetting and quality control processes.

Our Miami address (55 SW 9th ST APT 3806) is our dispatch office, not a walk-in storefront. We don't accept walk-in customers, but we're always available by email at support@firstclasscontractors.com and online through our website to schedule service and answer questions.

Compliance & Legitimacy

Miami Septic Pros operates in full compliance with Florida and Miami-Dade County regulations:

  • •Licensing Requirements: All contractors in our network hold valid Florida septic contractor licenses. We verify licensing status before adding contractors to our network and maintain records of all certifications.
  • •Miami-Dade Compliance: All work performed complies with Miami-Dade County Department of Environmental Resources Management (DERM) regulations, including permit requirements, system design standards, and installation codes.
  • •Insurance Coverage: All contractors maintain general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage. Insurance is verified before contractors are added to our network.
  • •Environmental Responsibility: We ensure proper waste disposal at licensed facilities and compliance with all environmental regulations. Contractors follow best practices to protect groundwater and prevent contamination.

When you work with Miami Septic Pros, you can trust that all services are performed by licensed professionals who meet or exceed regulatory requirements.
