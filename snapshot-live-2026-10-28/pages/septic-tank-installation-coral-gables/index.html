Professional Septic Tank Installation in Coral Gables

Miami Septic Pros provides complete septic tank installation services for new construction, property development, and system upgrades throughout Coral Gables and surrounding South Miami-Dade County communities. System Types and Miami-Dade Requirements

Effective January 1, 2023, Miami-Dade County implemented stricter standards through Ordinance 22-83, prohibiting conventional (Type 1) systems for new installations. All new systems in Coral Gables must meet Type 2 (secondary treatment), Type 3 (advanced secondary treatment), or Type 4 (Florida Keys Nutrient Reduction) standards depending on your property type, wastewater volume, and proximity to surface water bodies.

Critical design requirements include minimum 100 feet from surface water bodies, 50 feet from property lines, drainfield bottom at least 36 inches above wet season high water table, and a 50% reserve drainfield area for future expansion.

Installation Timeline in Coral Gables

Total timeline from initial site evaluation to completed installation typically ranges from 6-10 weeks. This includes site evaluation and design (1-2 weeks), DERM permitting (several weeks), installation (1-3 weeks), and post-installation inspection (1-2 weeks). Complex projects or permit delays can extend this timeframe.
