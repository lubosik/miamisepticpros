What Is Septic Tank Inspection?

Septic tank inspection is the professional evaluation of tank condition, function, and compliance, including visual inspection, level checks, component assessment, and system testing to identify problems and ensure proper operation. How Septic Tank Inspection Works

1. Tank location & access. Technicians locate the tank and ensure safe access, clearing debris or vegetation if needed. They check for risers or access points.

2. Visual inspection. Technicians inspect tank exterior, checking for cracks, damage, or signs of failure. They examine tank structure and condition.

3. Tank opening & interior inspection. Technicians open tank lids or risers, inspecting interior condition, checking sludge and scum levels, and examining tank components including baffles and outlets.

4. Level & function testing. Technicians check liquid levels, test system function, and assess wastewater flow. They identify any problems affecting tank operation.

5. Component assessment. Technicians evaluate all tank components including inlet and outlet pipes, baffles, and structural elements. They identify damage or wear requiring attention.

6. Documentation & recommendations. Technicians provide detailed inspection reports, document findings, and recommend maintenance, repairs, or follow-up services based on identified issues.
