What Is Camera Inspection?

Camera inspection uses a waterproof video camera mounted on a flexible cable to visually inspect the interior of septic system pipes, drain lines, and sometimes tank interiors. The camera transmits real-time video to a monitor, allowing technicians to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion, collapsed pipes, and other problems without digging. This diagnostic tool helps identify the exact location and nature of problems before expensive repairs begin.

The inspection process involves inserting the camera into an access point, typically through a cleanout, drain opening, or tank access riser. The camera is pushed through the pipe system while technicians monitor the video feed. Modern cameras include features like distance measurement, pan-and-tilt capabilities, and high-definition video recording for documentation. The entire inspection is recorded for review and documentation.

Camera inspection is particularly valuable for diagnosing recurring problems, locating blockages, verifying pipe condition, and planning repairs. In Hollywood, where many properties have older septic systems and complex drain line configurations, camera inspection helps avoid unnecessary excavation and provides accurate problem identification.

How Camera Inspection Works

The inspection process begins with locating access points to your septic system or drain lines. Licensed technicians insert a waterproof camera into the system through a cleanout, drain opening, or tank access riser. The camera is pushed through the pipes while technicians monitor the video feed in real-time.

As the camera moves through the system, technicians look for signs of problems including blockages, cracks, root intrusion, collapsed pipes, improper connections, and structural damage. Modern cameras include distance measurement capabilities, allowing technicians to pinpoint the exact location of problems. The entire inspection is recorded for documentation and review.

After the inspection, technicians provide a detailed report with video footage documenting any problems found. This report helps you understand system condition, plan necessary repairs, and make informed decisions about maintenance or replacement. If problems are identified, you may need septic tank repair in Hollywood or drainfield repair in Hollywood to address the issues.
