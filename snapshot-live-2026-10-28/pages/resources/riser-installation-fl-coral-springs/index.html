What Is Riser Installation?

Riser installation is a professional service that installs vertical pipes extending from your buried septic tank's access ports up to ground level, with sealed lids you can access without digging. Risers are made from PVC, polyethylene, or fiberglass and eliminate the need to excavate your lawn every time your tank needs service. They provide permanent, easy access to your septic tank for maintenance, pumping, and inspections.

The installation process typically includes locating your septic tank accurately, excavating to expose tank access ports, measuring depth to determine riser length needed, connecting riser pipe to tank access port with watertight seal, installing riser vertically to ground level, installing sealed lid at ground level, and restoring landscaping around installation area. Professional installation ensures risers are properly sealed, stable, and provide long-term access.

Riser installation is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself by eliminating excavation costs on future service visits. Most older septic systems in Broward County were installed without risers, making maintenance harder and more expensive. Installing risers protects your lawn from repeated digging damage and makes service faster and easier.

How Riser Installation Works

The installation process begins with locating your septic tank accurately. Licensed professionals use electronic locators, ground-penetrating radar, or manual probing to find your tank and identify access port locations. This initial step ensures risers are installed in the correct locations.

Next, professionals excavate to expose tank access ports. This involves carefully digging around the access ports to create space for riser installation. In Coral Springs, where properties often have well-maintained lawns, professionals take care to minimize damage and restore landscaping after installation.

Once access ports are exposed, professionals measure the depth to determine riser length needed. They then connect riser pipe to the tank access port with a watertight seal, install the riser vertically to ground level, and install a sealed lid at ground level. The riser must be properly sealed to prevent water infiltration and ensure long-term function.

After installation, professionals backfill around the riser for stability and restore landscaping around the installation area. If your tank needs related services, you may also need septic tank pumping in Coral Springs after riser installation, or septic inspection in Coral Springs to assess system condition.
