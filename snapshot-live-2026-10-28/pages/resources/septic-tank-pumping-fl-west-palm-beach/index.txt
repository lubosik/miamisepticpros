Real estate contracts frequently stipulate that the septic tank must be pumped and inspected within a set number of days before closing. Keeping up with a proactive pumping schedule means you have documentation ready for HOA boards, buyers, and lenders without last-minute rush fees.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed">Hurricane preparation is another critical consideration. Palm Beach County emergency management recommends pumping tanks before the peak of hurricane season, especially in flood-prone areas near the Intracoastal. If technicians discover structural damage during pumping, they coordinate with FDOH or Palm Beach County ERM for repair approvals.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed"><strong>2. Crews use as-built drawings, probes, metal detectors, or camera equipment to find lids without damaging hardscape.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed"><strong>3.
