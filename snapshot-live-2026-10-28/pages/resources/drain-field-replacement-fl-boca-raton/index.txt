<h2 id="why-you-need-drain-field-replacement-in-boca-raton" class="text-h2 font-serif-headings font-semibold text-charcoal mt-12 mb-4">Why You Need Drain Field Replacement in Boca Raton</h2><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed">Palm Beach County regulations require properly functioning drainfields in all septic systems. Replacement restores compliance and prevents costly fines or system shutdowns.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed">Groundwater protection is another critical consideration.
