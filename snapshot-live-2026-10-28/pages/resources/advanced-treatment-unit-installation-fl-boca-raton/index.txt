During rainy season (May through October) and high tides, groundwater rises close to the surface, reducing the separation between drainfields and the water table. Conventional systems may not provide adequate treatment in these conditions, making ATUs necessary to protect water quality.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed">Properties in neighborhoods like Mizner Park, Boca Raton Resort &amp; Club area, and communities near the FAU campus often face stricter environmental regulations due to their proximity to water bodies. Real estate contracts frequently stipulate that septic systems must meet current environmental standards. Installing an ATU ensures your property stays compliant and marketable.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed">Hurricane preparation is another critical consideration. Palm Beach County emergency management recommends ensuring septic systems have adequate treatment capacity before the peak of hurricane season. ATUs with proper maintenance can handle increased loads during power outages and storm events better than conventional systems.</p><h2 id="how-advanced-treatment-unit-installation-works" class="text-h2 font-serif-headings font-semibold text-charcoal mt-12 mb-4">How Advanced Treatment Unit Installation Works</h2><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed"><strong>1. ATU installations typically require both county and state approvals.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed"><strong>2. System design.</strong> Based on site conditions, household size, and local regulations, the contractor designs an ATU system that meets Palm Beach County requirements. This includes selecting the appropriate ATU model, sizing the unit, and designing the drainfield or dispersal system.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed"><strong>3. Excavation & installation.</strong> Crews excavate for the ATU tank, electrical connections, and distribution system. The ATU is carefully placed, leveled, and connected to the home's plumbing. Electrical service is installed for the aeration system, alarms, and controls.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed"><strong>4. Distribution system.</strong> The treated effluent is distributed to a drainfield, drip irrigation system, or other approved dispersal method. The distribution system is designed to handle the higher-quality effluent from the ATU.</p><p class="text-body text-body-text mb-6 leading-relaxed"><strong>5.
