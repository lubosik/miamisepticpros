What Is Drainfield Replacement?

Drainfield replacement is a major septic system project that involves completely removing and replacing a failed drainfield with a new system. Unlike drainfield repair which fixes specific problems, replacement is necessary when the drainfield has failed completely and cannot be restored through repairs. The replacement process includes removing the old drainfield, preparing the site, installing new distribution pipes, and ensuring proper soil absorption.

The replacement process typically includes site evaluation to determine the best location for the new drainfield, obtaining required permits from Broward County Environmental Protection, excavating and removing the old drainfield system, preparing the soil and site for the new system, installing new distribution pipes and components, and testing the new system to ensure proper function. Professional replacement ensures the new drainfield meets all regulatory requirements and provides long-term function.

Drainfield replacement is significantly more expensive than repair, typically costing $10,000-$25,000 or more depending on system size, site conditions, and local regulations. In Wilton Manors, where properties are often close together and space is limited, replacement can be particularly challenging and expensive. However, replacement is necessary when repair is no longer possible and provides a long-term solution for system function.

How Drainfield Replacement Works

The replacement process begins with site evaluation to determine the best location for the new drainfield. Licensed professionals assess soil conditions, groundwater levels, available space, and regulatory requirements. This evaluation helps determine the appropriate drainfield design and ensures the new system will function properly.

Next, professionals obtain required permits from Broward County Environmental Protection. Drainfield replacement requires permits for excavation, system installation, and environmental compliance. The permit process includes submitting site plans, system designs, and environmental assessments. Professional contractors handle the permit process to ensure compliance.

Once permits are obtained, professionals excavate and remove the old drainfield. This includes safely removing distribution pipes, gravel, and any contaminated soil. The old drainfield materials must be properly disposed of according to Broward County regulations. If your septic tank also needs work, you may need septic tank repair in Wilton Manors or septic tank replacement in Wilton Manors as part of the replacement project.

After the old drainfield is removed, professionals prepare the site and install the new drainfield. This includes excavating to the proper depth, preparing the soil, installing new distribution pipes and gravel, and backfilling. The new drainfield must meet current Broward County standards and be properly installed to ensure long-term function.
