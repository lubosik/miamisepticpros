How often should I pump my septic tank?+

Most septic tanks should be pumped every 3-5 years, depending on tank size, household size, and water usage. For Miami-Dade County properties, we recommend pumping every 3 years due to the high water table and frequent heavy rainfall. Regular pumping prevents backups and extends the life of your drainfield.

What are the signs that my septic tank needs pumping?+

Common signs include slow-draining sinks and toilets, gurgling sounds in drains, sewage odors in your yard or home, water backing up in toilets or drains, and lush green grass over the drainfield. If you notice any of these signs, schedule a pumping service immediately.

How much does septic tank pumping cost?+

Septic tank pumping costs typically range from $300-$600 in Miami-Dade County, depending on tank size, accessibility, and location. We provide upfront, transparent pricing with no hidden fees. Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote specific to your property.

Do you offer emergency septic services?+

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency septic services for backups, overflows, and urgent issues. Our emergency response team can typically arrive within hours, not days. Emergency service is available throughout Miami-Dade County and surrounding areas.

How long does septic tank pumping take?+

Most septic tank pumping services take 1-2 hours, depending on tank size and accessibility. This includes pumping, inspection, and cleanup. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily routine.

What should I do if my septic system backs up?+

If your septic system backs up, stop using water immediately to prevent further overflow. Do not use toilets, sinks, or washing machines. Call us immediately for emergency service. Avoid using chemical drain cleaners, as they can damage your system. We can typically respond within hours.

Do I need a permit for septic system repairs or installation?+

Yes, most septic system repairs, replacements, and new installations require permits from the Florida Department of Health or Miami-Dade County DERM. Our licensed contractors handle all permit applications and ensure compliance with local regulations. We guide you through the entire process.

How do I know if my drainfield is failing?+

Signs of drainfield failure include standing water or soggy areas over the drainfield, sewage odors, slow-draining fixtures throughout the house, and sewage backing up into the home. If you notice these signs, contact us immediately for an inspection and repair estimate.

Can you locate my septic tank if I don't know where it is?+

Yes, we offer septic tank location services using specialized equipment to find buried tanks. This service is essential before pumping, repairs, or property transactions. We can typically locate your tank within 30-60 minutes.

What is included in a septic system inspection?+

A comprehensive septic inspection includes checking the tank for cracks, leaks, and proper water levels; inspecting inlet and outlet baffles; examining the drainfield for signs of failure; testing the distribution box (if present); and checking for proper system operation. We provide a detailed written report.
