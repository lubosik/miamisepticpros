<p>Sludge digesters are treatment systems breaking down organic matter in wastewater sludge through biological anaerobic processes reducing sludge volume by nearly 50% while producing biogas as renewable energy source. <h3>How do sludge digesters and bio-treatment units work?</h3>

<p>Sludge digesters use four-stage anaerobic process including hydrolysis breaking down complex materials, acidogenesis creating acids, acetogenesis producing intermediate products, and methanogenesis creating biogas. Bio-treatment units use aerobic process with oxygen-rich environment created by mechanical blowers or compressors supporting specialized bacteria breaking down waste more efficiently than anaerobic systems, producing superior effluent quality meeting advanced treatment standards.</p>
