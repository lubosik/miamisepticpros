<h3>How does sewer jetting work?</h3>

<p>Sewer jetting works by inserting specialized nozzle into sewer or drain line positioned to release highly pressurized water in multiple directions, with water stream cutting through grease buildup, sludge, and mineral deposits while simultaneously flushing debris completely from system. Pressurized water particularly effective at cutting through tree roots and other tough obstructions plaguing aging sewer systems, cleaning entire pipe diameter and removing all buildup ensuring thorough cleaning and flow restoration.</p>

<h3>What does sewer jetting involve?</h3>

<p>Professional sewer jetting involves hydro-jetting machines capable of cleaning pipe diameters ranging from 2" to 16" with runs extending up to 500 feet addressing both residential branch lines and large municipal sewer mains. Service includes complementary sewer camera inspections providing real-time diagnostics without requiring excavation, comprehensive cleaning removing all deposits, debris removal, flow restoration, system assessment, and CIPP technology for trenchless pipe repair when structural damage discovered.</p>

<h3>What are benefits of professional sewer jetting?</h3>

<p>Professional sewer jetting benefits include non-invasive and non-destructive nature preserving floors, landscaping, and walls by working through existing access points, comprehensive cleaning removing all deposits from pipe walls ensuring longer-lasting results, pipe restoration for cast iron pipes removing scale and rust restoring full flow capacity, environmentally friendly water-only approach eliminating contamination concerns, bacteria removal improving overall system health, and prevention-focused routine jetting preventing costly repairs extending system lifespan.</p>

<h3>When is sewer jetting needed versus other methods?</h3>

<p>Sewer jetting appropriate when comprehensive cleaning needed removing all deposits, severe blockages requiring high-pressure removal, root intrusion needing cutting removal, preventive maintenance maintaining system function, and when non-invasive method preferred avoiding excavation. Jetting superior to chemical cleaners which can damage pipes, mechanical snaking which doesn't remove buildup, and excavation methods which cause property damage, with single professional application delaying subsequent clearing needs up to four times longer than traditional snaking methods.</p>
